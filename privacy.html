Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Jack Bauer, a personal assistant operated for Som da Bíblia, handles TikTok information when the account owner chooses to connect TikTok to the service.

Information accessed

Jack Bauer accesses only the TikTok information authorized by the account owner, such as basic profile data and video information. The specific access depends on the permissions the owner approves in TikTok.

How information is used

Information is used only to help the account owner review TikTok profile and video performance, organize content work, and respond to the owner’s requests. Jack Bauer does not publish, edit, delete, or otherwise manage TikTok content without the owner’s explicit approval.

Credentials and sharing

Passwords and API secrets are not requested in chat or intentionally stored in these pages. Authorization is handled through the applicable TikTok and integration-provider authorization flow. Information is not sold or used for advertising.

Retention and revocation

The account owner may revoke TikTok authorization at any time through TikTok or the connected integration service. Access is then no longer used for new requests.

Contact

For questions about this policy, open an issue in this repository.

This notice applies to this personal TikTok integration and may be updated if the integration’s scope changes.
